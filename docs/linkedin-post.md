I got tired of typing my availability into emails.

So I built a Thunderbird add-on with Claude over a few evenings: one button in the compose window. It reads my calendars, lists my free slots in the recipient's time zone (PT, ET, CET, whatever they live in) and drops them where the cursor is. Second tab: type a time like "Tue 15:00", and it sends the calendar invitation to everyone in the thread, with a Google Meet link.

It started as a 40-line free-slot calculator. The invitation part came from one question: why can't I send the invite from where I type?
