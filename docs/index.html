Compose Calendar

Free slots and calendar invitations, right in the Thunderbird compose window.

Download the add-on View on GitHub

Thunderbird 115+. After downloading: Add-ons Manager → gear icon → Install Add-on From File.

  • Availability. Your free slots from every calendar, in the recipient's time zone, inserted at the cursor.
  • Invitation. Attendees prefilled from the whole thread; type the time like you'd say it; a Google Meet link is added.
  • Private. Runs entirely inside Thunderbird. No accounts, no tracking, nothing leaves your machine.
